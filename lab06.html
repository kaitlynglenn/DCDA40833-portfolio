Interactive Hometown Map

Lab Requirements

This lab required creating an interactive hometown map by combining cartographic design and web mapping technology. I designed a custom, art-inspired basemap in Mapbox Studio, created a dataset of meaningful hometown locations, and used Python to integrate the data into an interactive map. The assignment focused on blending creative map design with technical implementation to build a personalized web-based visualization.

Reflection & Analysis

Design Inspiration

For this portion of the lab, I used an image by RLN Studios called "Giverny Morning Hues”. It is a beautiful landscape painting that has beautiful colors that I thought would work very well with my interactive map. It worked well that I could base the colors of the land and water off the colors in the painting to still look natural. I used some of the brighter colors in the painting for the streets, labels, and location markers.
